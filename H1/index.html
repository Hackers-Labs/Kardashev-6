Challenges in Enforcement

The Problem

Under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules 2011, every retail packaged commodity must carry 6 mandatory declarations.

Mandatory Packaging Declarations

  • • Manufacturer / Packer / Importer Name & Postal Address
  • • Generic / Common Product Name
  • • Net Quantity in standard units of weight or measure
  • • Maximum Retail Price (MRP) incl. of all taxes
  • • Month & Year of Manufacture / Packing / Import
  • • Consumer Care Cell Phone Number & Email Address

Manual Inspection Limitations

  • ✕ Time-consuming manual checking of fine font sizes
  • ✕ Human error in identifying missing email or address lines
  • ✕ Inconsistent paper-based inspection records across regions
  • ✕ Difficulty tracking historical violations for repeat offenders
  • ✕ Delayed central report generation for ministry submission
