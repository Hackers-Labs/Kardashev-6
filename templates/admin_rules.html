Legal Metrology Enforcement Manual & Penalties Scale

Statutory standards under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 & Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 for enforcement officers.

Rule 6: Mandatory Declarations on Pre-Packaged Commodities

Sub-Rule Mandatory Declaration Statutory Format & Guidelines Severity Level
Rule 6(1)(a) Name & Address of Manufacturer / Packer / Importer Must include full street address, premise name, city, and PIN code. If manufactured by a third party, both manufacturer and marketer/packer must be clearly stated. Critical
Rule 6(1)(b) Common or Generic Name of the Commodity Must clearly indicate the generic identity of the goods contained (not solely the brand name). High
Rule 6(1)(c) Net Quantity in Standard Metric Units Weight (g, kg), Volume (ml, l), or Count (number). Must strictly follow standard SI symbols (no capitalization of 'kg', 'g', etc.). Minimum font height rules apply. Critical
Rule 6(1)(d) Month and Year of Manufacture / Packing / Import In standard format (e.g. MM/YYYY or Month YYYY). Words like "Packed On", "Mfg Date", or "Imported On" must precede. Critical
Rule 6(1)(e) Maximum Retail Price (MRP) In format: MRP ₹ xx.xx (incl. of all taxes). Must include unit sale price (per g / per ml / per piece) as mandated by the 2022 Amendment. Critical
Rule 6(1)(n) Consumer Care Contact Details Designation of officer, street address, telephone / toll-free helpline, and official email address for consumer grievances. High
Rule 6(10) Country of Origin Mandatory for all imported packages and commodities sold through retail or e-commerce marketplaces in India. High

Statutory Penalties & Compounding Powers (Legal Metrology Act, 2009)

Section 36(1) • Non-Standard Pre-Packaged Goods

Whoever manufactures, packs, imports, sells, or distributes any non-standard package not conforming to Rule 6 declarations:

  • First Offence: Fine up to ₹25,000
  • Second Offence: Fine up to ₹50,000
  • Subsequent Offences: Fine up to ₹1,00,000 or imprisonment up to 1 year, or both.
Section 48 • Compounding of Offences

Powers conferred upon Assistant Controller / Authorized Supervisory Officers:

  • Offences under Section 36 may be compounded before or after the institution of prosecution.
  • Compounding sum shall not exceed the maximum fine prescribed under the respective section.
  • Once compounded, no further proceedings shall be taken against the offender in respect of that offence.
