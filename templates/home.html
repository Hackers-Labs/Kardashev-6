Statutory Coverage

Statutory Declarations Evaluated Under Rule 6

Every pre-packaged retail commodity undergoes rigorous automated compliance checks across 40+ statutory guidelines.

Manufacturer & Packer Identity

Strict validation of manufacturer, packer, or importer name and full physical address with pin code and country of origin.

MRP & Unit Sale Price (USP)

Checks for mandatory "Inclusive of all taxes" declaration, standard currency symbol (₹), and statutory Unit Sale Price per g/ml.

Net Quantity & Standard Units

Validates standard metric units (g, kg, ml, l) as mandated under Second Schedule without deceptive packaging.

Consumer Care Redressal

Ensures presence of working customer support helpline telephone, email ID, and designation of the redressal officer.

Month & Year of Packing / Expiry

Verification of Manufacturing Date, Packing Date, Expiry / Best Before timeframe, and batch/lot identification numbers.

FSSAI & Food Labelling

Validates 14-digit FSSAI license numbers, green/brown vegetarian logo symbols, allergen declarations, and nutritional facts.

Enforcement Workflow

3-Stage Inspection & Statutory Notice Generation

Designed for field officers during market surveillance, seizure operations, and consumer grievance investigations.

01

Image Capture

Officer photographs product packaging or uploads evidence files directly from device or field camera.

02

AI OCR & Rule Audit

Tesseract & Gemini Vision automatically detect, extract, and check mandatory declarations against 40+ statutory rules.

03

Legal Action & Export

Inspector confirms verdict and downloads official sealed PDF certificate and editable Word (.docx) violation notice.
